Common questions

What is an ATS, and why does it matter?

An applicant tracking system (ATS) scans CVs for keywords and structure before a recruiter reads them. I match your CV to each job's wording and test it so it gets through.

How long does it take?

Ready-to-send files are delivered within 48 hours.

What do I need to send?

The job description you're targeting and your current CV. If you don't have a CV, a short summary of your roles and achievements is enough to start from scratch. You can send both on WhatsApp.

Is my CV kept confidential?

Yes. Your CV and job descriptions are used only to prepare your documents and are not shared. See the privacy note in the contact section.

What is the XYZ formula?

A way of writing achievements: what you accomplished (X), how it was measured (Y), and how you did it (Z). It turns duties into measurable impact.

I'm a fresh graduate with little experience. Can you help?

Yes. I turn internships, academic projects, volunteering and part-time work into achievements, and match the CV to the job you are applying for. Resume Essentials is a good place to start.

What does the 30-day window cover?

Resume Plus + ATS includes 2 tailored CVs for your next 2 job applications within 30 days. Executive includes unlimited tailored CVs for 30 days.

How do revisions work?

Resume Plus + ATS includes 1 revision round, and Executive includes 2.
