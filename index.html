FAQ

Common questions

What is an ATS, and why does it matter?

An applicant tracking system (ATS) scans CVs for keywords and structure before a recruiter reads them. I match your CV to each job's wording and test it so it gets through.

How long does it take?

Ready-to-send files are delivered within 48 hours.

What do I need to send?

The job description you're targeting and your current CV. If you don't have a CV, a short summary of your roles and achievements is enough to start from scratch.

What is the XYZ formula?

It's a way of writing achievements: what you accomplished (X), how it was measured (Y), and how you did it (Z). It turns duties into measurable impact.

How do revisions work?

Resume Plus + ATS includes 1 revision round, and Executive includes 2.

Are the prices fixed?

The prices shown are approximate guides. Message me for a quote tailored to your role and level.
